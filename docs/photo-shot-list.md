# How to photograph the cinnamon rolls (for Haaans)

You don't need a fancy camera. Your phone is perfect. Here's exactly what to do.

## What to grab first

- Your phone
- A big piece of white posterboard (or any big white poster/foam board from a craft store)
- A table near a window

## Set up your spot (do this once)

1. Put the white posterboard flat on the table, pushed up against a window so daylight is coming in from one side.
2. Put the pan of rolls on top of the posterboard, roughly in the middle.
3. Stand facing the pan so the light from the window is coming from your left or right side (not from behind you, and not from behind the pan).
4. Look down at the pan like you're checking on it — not straight down from above, just a little bit, like a slight tilt. Somewhere around a "you're standing and glancing down" angle feels right.
5. Once you're standing in a spot that looks good on your screen, remember where your feet are. Maybe even mark the spot on the floor with a piece of tape.

## Taking the photos

Take one photo per flavor, always from that exact same spot, without moving your feet:

1. Original
2. Ham & Cheese
3. Birthday Cake
4. Oreo

Between each photo, only the pan changes — you stay exactly where you are, phone held exactly the same way. This matters more than anything else on this list: if you shift even a little, the photos won't line up right on the website.

Also take:

5. One photo of the frosting tub, lid off, sitting on the same white posterboard.

## A few things to double check before you take each photo

- Is the whole pan visible in the frame, with a little bit of white space around it on all sides?
- Is the background actually plain white poster board — no clutter, no hands, no other stuff on the table?
- Does the lighting look even, without a big dark shadow across the rolls?
- Is the photo in focus (not blurry)?

## Sending them to us

- Don't crop the photos. Send them exactly as your phone saved them.
- Send all 5 in one text or email so they stay together.
- If a photo looks blurry or too dark, it's totally fine to retake it — better to send a good one a minute later than a rushed one now.

That's it. Five photos, same spot, same angle, one per flavor plus the frosting tub.
